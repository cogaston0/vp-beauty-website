01

Nasolabial Fillers (Hyaluronic Acid)

Treatment Overview

Smooth and soften the nasolabial folds (smile lines) with our premium hyaluronic acid fillers. This non-surgical treatment restores volume and creates a more youthful appearance by filling in creases that deepen with age.

Benefits

  • Reduces appearance of smile lines
  • Restores facial volume and fullness
  • Natural-looking, gradual results
  • Immediate improvement with no downtime
  • Results last 6-12 months
  • Hyaluronic acid is naturally found in skin

Process

After consultation to determine your goals, the treatment area is gently numbed. Our specialist carefully injects premium hyaluronic acid filler into the nasolabial folds, sculpting and smoothing as they work. The injection creates subtle, natural-looking results that enhance your features while maintaining facial expression.

Duration: 30-45 minutes

PRP Platelet

Treatment Overview

Experience the power of Platelet Rich Plasma (PRP) therapy, a cutting-edge regenerative treatment that uses your own blood's healing properties to rejuvenate facial skin. This natural treatment stimulates collagen and improves skin quality.

Benefits

  • Natural regenerative therapy using your own cells
  • Stimulates collagen and elastin production
  • Improves skin texture and tone
  • Reduces fine lines and wrinkles
  • Creates youthful, glowing skin
  • Results improve over time with natural collagen growth

Process

We start by collecting a small amount of your blood, which is then processed to extract the platelet-rich plasma. This concentrated serum, rich with growth factors, is carefully applied to your facial skin through micro-needling or injection. The growth factors stimulate your body's natural healing response, promoting new collagen and improving overall skin quality.
