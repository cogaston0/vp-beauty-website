Nasolabial Fillers (Hyaluronic Acid)

Treatment Overview

Smooth and soften the nasolabial folds (smile lines) with our premium hyaluronic acid fillers. This non-surgical treatment restores volume and creates a more youthful appearance by filling in creases that deepen with age.

Benefits

  • Reduces appearance of smile lines
  • Restores facial volume and fullness
  • Natural-looking, gradual results
  • Immediate improvement with no downtime
  • Results last 6-12 months
  • Hyaluronic acid is naturally found in skin

Process

After consultation to determine your goals, the treatment area is gently numbed. Our specialist carefully injects premium hyaluronic acid filler into the nasolabial folds, sculpting and smoothing as they work.

Duration: 30-45 minutes
