01

Cleaning + Vitamin Crashes (Dehydration)

Treatment Overview

Our signature vitamin crash treatment is specifically designed to combat dehydration and restore your skin's natural moisture balance. This intensive facial combines deep cleansing with a concentrated vitamin infusion to revitalize dull, dehydrated skin.

Benefits

  • Intense hydration and moisture replenishment
  • Brightens dull, tired-looking skin
  • Reduces fine lines caused by dehydration
  • Improves skin elasticity and plumpness
  • Visible results after just one treatment

Process

The treatment begins with a gentle cleanse to remove impurities, followed by a specialized exfoliation. We then apply a potent vitamin complex that penetrates deeply into the skin layers, delivering essential nutrients and moisture. The session concludes with a hydrating mask and protective serum application.
